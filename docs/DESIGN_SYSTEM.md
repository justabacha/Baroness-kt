\# Baroness Design System



\## Colors

\- Primary: `#FF6B6B` (coral)

\- Background: `#121212` (dark)

\- Surface: `#1E1E1E` (elevated dark)

\- Text Primary: `#FFFFFF`

\- Text Secondary: `#B3B3B3`



\## Spacing

\- Base unit: 8dp

\- Screen padding: 16dp

\- Card padding: 12dp

\- Icon size: 24dp



\## Typography

\- Headlines: 24sp, Bold

\- Body: 16sp, Regular

\- Captions: 12sp, Regular



\## Components

\- Cards: 12dp corner radius, 4dp elevation

\- Buttons: 8dp corner radius, full width

\- Modals: 16dp corner radius, slide up animation
