## Issue #1: Missing Timestamp-based Conflict Resolution
### Current State
Room used `OnConflictStrategy.REPLACE` blindly, overwriting local changes with potentially stale remote data.

### Proposed Change
Modified `WishEntity` to include `updatedAt`. Updated `WishDao` with `upsertWithTimestampCheck` to ensure newer data wins. Incremented database version to 3 in `AppDatabase.kt`.

### Why This Approach
Prevents data loss during offline-to-online transitions where remote updates might arrive after local edits.

### Risks
Potential performance impact of transaction-based upserts, mitigated by batching.

### Files to Touch
- `app/src/main/java/com/baroness/app/data/local/database/WishEntity.kt`
- `app/src/main/java/com/baroness/app/data/local/dao/WishDao.kt`
